## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a human-first SaaS identity crafted specifically for independent professionals, boutique agencies, and agile service teams. It actively rejects the stale, overly dense, and intimidating aesthetics of legacy enterprise software in favor of warmth, effortless clarity, and approachable professionalism.

### Brand Personality
- **Warm & Empathetic:** The interface feels like a collaborative partner, not an administrative burden. Interactions are forgiving, conversational, and direct.
- **Dynamic & Agile:** Emphasizes rapid creation workflows—turning inquiries into accepted quotations in minutes with zero visual friction.
- **Trustworthy & Grounded:** Balances casual closeness with the precision and dependability required for commercial contracts and financial agreements.

### Design Style: Warm Contemporary Modern
The visual language merges modern clean layouts with tactile softness:
- **Soft tactile surfaces:** Pure white functional cards suspended above delicate, warm-tinted neutral canvases.
- **Human accents:** Energetic warm highlights (amber/coral) deliberately break monotone enterprise patterns to celebrate wins (accepted quotes, milestones, sent documents).
- **Friendly Geometry:** Curvature is generous and organic without feeling childish, offering high touchability and intuitive visual chunking.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to provide visual confidence, clarity, and inviting touchpoints throughout the quotation lifecycle.

### Key Roles
- **Primary (`#4F46E5` - Electric Indigo):** Represents the connective tissue—actions, core navigation, primary conversion buttons, and active workflow states.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B` - Warm Amber):** Introduces warmth, optimism, and attention for pending quotes, calls-to-action that require user review, and celebratory micro-interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Crisp Emerald):** Used purposefully for financial positive states: accepted proposals, paid invoices, and conversion success metrics.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Slate Black):** Anchors deep typographic contrast, grounding the friendly design with reliable legibility.

### Surface & Border Architecture
- **App Canvas:** `#FAF9F6` (Alabaster cream) to `#F8FAFC` (Slate mist), delivering an organic paper-like warmth that softens prolonged screen time.
- **Card & Surface Layers:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white) to ensure crisp focal definition.
- **Structural Borders:** Subtle slate dividers (`#E2E8F0` at 100% or `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`), maintaining clear spatial limits without harsh visual interruption.

## Typography

Typographic choices are driven by the geometric yet humanist rhythm of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Its rounded terminals and wide apertures provide high readability on density-sensitive views like budget line items and pipeline breakdowns.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Display & Headings:** Use tight letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and bolder weights (`700`, `800`) to present project numbers, total values, and key headers with friendly assertiveness.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` across all quote amounts, currency inputs, and data tables to preserve clean vertical alignment.
- **Labels & Badges:** `label-sm` utilizes uppercase rendering with loose tracking (`0.04em`) to establish crisp status indicators (e.g., `BORRADOR`, `ENVIADO`, `ACEPTADO`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column responsive grid with a strict 4px base increment rhythm to achieve visual order without appearing rigid.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, 16px page margins, 12px column gutters. Primary actions dock into fixed or floating bottom app bars for easy thumb access.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, 24px margins, 16px gutters. Split-screen creation modes (editor on the left, live preview on the right) fold into progressive tabs.
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout with a constrained max content container (`1216px` / `76rem`). Sidebar navigation is persistent (240px width), with modular card panels filling the remaining space.

### Structural Rhythm
- **Micro Spacing (4px - 8px):** Within atomic elements (icons next to text, input labels to form fields).
- **Component Spacing (16px - 24px):** Interior padding of proposal cards, customer sheets, and table rows.
- **Section Spacing (32px - 48px):** Distance separating independent operational modules (e.g., Quote Overview vs. Payment Conditions).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses ambient, warm-tinted elevation over rigid geometric drop shadows to create a buoyant, inviting environment.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat):** Base canvas and embedded sub-panels. Rendered flat against `#FAF9F6` with a delicate outline (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Level 1 (Default Cards & Items):**
  - Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`
  - Used for standard dashboard widgets, CRM deal tiles, and line items.
- **Level 2 (Interactive & Hovered Cards):**
  - Shadow: `0 10px 20px -5px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`
  - Subtle upward translation (`transform: translateY(-2px)`) on hover to provide responsive feedback.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Quote Previews):**
  - Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`
  - Backdrop: Soft blurred scrim (`backdrop-filter: blur(6px); background: rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The shape system reinforces approachability. Sharp 90-degree corners are eliminated from interaction surfaces to avoid cold, legacy vibes.

### Curvature Standard
- **Inputs, Chips, and Buttons:** `rounded-xl` (12px to 14px) for high tactile friendliness and balanced proportions.
- **Cards, Panels, and Containers:** `rounded-2xl` (16px to 20px) to comfortably wrap dense item listings, preview panes, and metric groups.
- **Pills / Status Badges:** Fully rounded (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for categorical metadata, deal stages, and price tags.
- **Floating Overlays & Sheets:** `rounded-3xl` (24px) for bottom sheets on mobile devices and modal review modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#4F46E5`, text white, `rounded-xl`, height 44px (touch-friendly). Hover state deepens to `#4338CA` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 14px 0 rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.35)`).
- **Warm / Conversion Accent:** Used for "Enviar Cotización" or "Aceptar Presupuesto". Background `#F59E0B`, text `#0F172A` (bold contrast), active shadow tinted with amber.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, text `#4F46E5` or `#64748B`, hover with 5% tinted fill.

### Input Fields & Price Pickers
- **Style:** Height 44px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, `rounded-xl`.
- **Focus State:** Border changes to `#4F46E5` accompanied by a soft halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`.
- **Currency & Unit Steppers:** Integrated leading prefix icons (`€`, `$`) styled in muted slate with tabular text styling.

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Draft:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`.
- **Sent / Under Review:** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, left dot in `#F59E0B`.
- **Approved / Won:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#047857`, left dot in `#10B981`.
- **Declined:** Background `#FEE2E2`, text `#B91C1C`.
- **Geometry:** Height 24px, padding 4px 10px, `rounded-full`, typography `label-sm`.

### Quotation Line-Item Table / List
- Lightweight borders separating items, removing heavy grid cell borders.
- Reorder handles on the left (light slate, appearing on hover).
- Row hover triggers an immediate soft background shift (`#F8FAFC`).

### Endorsement Badge ("Un producto de Gallegeeks")
- Subtle and refined: small lockup placed in the footer or workspace corner.
- Monochromatic or subdued brand tint with a small warm geometric marker, maintaining autonomy while honoring heritage.
